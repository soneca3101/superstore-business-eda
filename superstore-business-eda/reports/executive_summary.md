# Executive Summary — Superstore Sales EDA

## Business Context

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify opportunities to improve
profitability, optimize discount strategy, and understand performance differences
across categories, regions, segments, and shipping modes.

## Key Findings

1. High sales do not always translate into high profit.
2. Some sub-categories generate negative profit and should be reviewed.
3. Higher discount levels are associated with weaker profitability.
4. Profitability varies significantly across regions and customer segments.
5. Shipping modes should be evaluated not only by speed but also by margin impact.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Implement discount governance

Create discount thresholds by product category and require approval for discounts
above a defined limit.

### 2. Review unprofitable sub-categories

Analyze pricing, supplier cost, and promotional strategy for sub-categories with
negative profit.

### 3. Use profit margin as a core KPI

Track profit margin by category, region, segment, and discount tier.

### 4. Optimize regional strategy

Investigate regions with high sales but low profit to identify issues related to
discounting, product mix, or operational inefficiency.

### 5. Balance shipping speed and profitability

Evaluate whether faster shipping methods are reducing margins and redesign
shipping policies accordingly.

## Final Conclusion

The analysis suggests that Superstore's main opportunity is not simply to sell more,
but to sell more profitably. By improving discount control, monitoring profit margin,
and reviewing unprofitable sub-categories, the company can increase profitability
without necessarily increasing sales volume.
